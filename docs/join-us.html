Working with AIM

AIM offers a collaborative research setting at the interface of AI, applied mathematics and mechanics, with participating groups based in Grenoble, France. The research combines theory, simulations and experiments and is oriented toward physically grounded and reliable modelling.

Prospective PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and scientific collaborators can contact the principal investigators to discuss potential opportunities and project fit.
