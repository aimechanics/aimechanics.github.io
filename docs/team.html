Participating groups

  • TRIPOP — Inria Grenoble: modelling, simulation and control of nonsmooth dynamical systems.
  • THOTH — Inria: mathematical and computational expertise in machine learning.
  • 3SR — Université Grenoble Alpes: experimental and theoretical geomechanics.
  • ECRINS — INRAE / IGE: granular and geophysical flows, with applications to natural hazards.

The participating researchers and their roles are drawn from the official chair description and Inria's activity report. This page can be extended with PhD researchers, postdoctoral fellows and collaborators as their affiliations are confirmed.
