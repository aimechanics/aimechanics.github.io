Beyond the research programme

Activities & open science

AIM is designed to connect methodological research, reproducible scientific software and interdisciplinary education.

Scientific software

The chair aims to support an innovation consortium dedicated to open-source software. Reproducibility and reuse are central to the goal of developing physically grounded computational tools that can be evaluated across numerical and experimental settings.

Related existing open-source work includes the NICE repository, TANN-multiscale and Siconos. These links provide background tools and are not presented as new AIM deliverables.

Training & interdisciplinary exchange

AIM plans an interdisciplinary training programme at the intersection of artificial intelligence and mechanics, bringing together perspectives from machine learning, physical modelling, experiments and numerical simulation.

Collaboration & dissemination

The collaboration spans the TRIPOP and THOTH Inria teams, the 3SR geomechanics group at Université Grenoble Alpes, and the ECRINS group at INRAE–IGE. Publications, workshops and publicly available resources can be added here as project outputs become available.
